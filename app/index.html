      About our sources

      Every trophy's name, description and grade comes from the official PS5 trophy list as published on Exophase, frozen on 18 November 2026. PSNProfiles was checked against the same list and agrees on every name, description and grade; where PSN tracks a count (for example 42 Shooting Range challenges or 250 stunts), that count is shown as the trophy's progress target.

      Each guide is written in our own words from the official description alone. We do not invent locations, steps or trophy names, and we do not copy other guides. Where a guide gives advice beyond the description, it says so and labels it as not yet checked in the game. Missable flags are cautious inferences from the wording of the official descriptions — for example a choice-driven or second-attempt description — and are labelled as inferred, not verified.

      Because the game had not yet been played for this guide, no gameplay claim here is presented as verified in the game. That honesty is deliberate: a cited, cautious guide beats a detailed but uncited one.
