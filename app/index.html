Roadmap to the platinum

Five stages, in order, built from the official descriptions: which story chapters and missions, which side strands, which collectibles and counts. About 200 hours for the platinum, by PowerPyx's estimate — long, not hard.

    Save advice: GTA 6 has no mission replay and no New Game Plus (per Outputlag's launch guide), so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy in the list is reported as possibly missable. The rest stay earnable after the credits — but with no mission replay, a manual save at each chapter start is cheap insurance.

    Three warnings for a trophy run

    • Cheats switch trophies off. GTABase and PowerPyx report that turning on a cheat disables trophies and saving — keep cheats off on your trophy save.
    • No mission replay. Outputlag's launch guide reports there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.
    • No online trophies. All 55 are single-player and offline, so nothing here needs another player.
